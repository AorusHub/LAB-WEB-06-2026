WHAT IS BIRTHDAY.EXE?

BIRTHDAY.EXE is a digital birthday experience designed to turn a simple birthday message into something more personal, playful, and interactive.

Instead of sending another ordinary birthday card, you can create an experience filled with messages, memories, mini-games, surprises, and little moments made specifically for someone special.
